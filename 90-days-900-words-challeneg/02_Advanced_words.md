#### ��� 5 Vocabulary Words

**1) Adapt**

* **Meaning (English):** To adjust to new conditions
* **Meaning (Urdu):** خود کو ڈھالنا / حالات کے مطابق بدلنا
* **Example:** You must adapt to new technologies in the DevOps field.

---

**2) Analyze**

* **Meaning (English):** To examine something carefully
* **Meaning (Urdu):** تجزیہ کرنا
* **Example:** The team needs to analyze the logs to find the issue.

---

**3) Approach**

* **Meaning (English):** A way of dealing with something
* **Meaning (Urdu):** طریقہ کار / انداز
* **Example:** We need a better approach to handle system failures.

---

**4) Significant**

* **Meaning (English):** Important or noticeable
* **Meaning (Urdu):** اہم / نمایاں
* **Example:** There was a significant improvement in system performance.

---

**5) Resolve**

* **Meaning (English):** To fix or solve a problem
* **Meaning (Urdu):** حل کرنا
* **Example:** The engineer resolved the server issue quickly.

---

#### ���️ Idiom of the Day

**Hit the nail on the head**

* **Meaning (English):** To say exactly the right thing
* **Meaning (Urdu):** بالکل درست بات کہنا
* **Example:** You hit the nail on the head with your analysis.

---

#### ��� Phrasal Verb of the Day

**Carry out**

* **Meaning (English):** To perform or complete a task
* **Meaning (Urdu):** انجام دینا / مکمل کرنا
* **Examples:**

  * The team carried out the deployment successfully.
  * We need to carry out testing before release.
