### 📅 Day 3 — Vocabulary Training (Moving toward B2)

Today we’ll push slightly more toward **professional + expressive English**.

---

#### 🔤 5 Vocabulary Words

**1) Implement**

* **Meaning (English):** To put a plan or system into action
* **Meaning (Urdu):** نافذ کرنا / عمل میں لانا
* **Example:** We need to implement a better monitoring system.

---

**2) Enhance**

* **Meaning (English):** To improve or increase quality/value
* **Meaning (Urdu):** بہتر بنانا / اضافہ کرنا
* **Example:** This update will enhance application performance.

---

**3) Complex**

* **Meaning (English):** Complicated or not simple
* **Meaning (Urdu):** پیچیدہ
* **Example:** This is a complex infrastructure setup.

---

**4) Reliable**

* **Meaning (English):** Someone or something you can trust
* **Meaning (Urdu):** قابلِ اعتماد
* **Example:** We need a reliable backup solution.

---

**5) Strategy**

* **Meaning (English):** A plan to achieve a goal
* **Meaning (Urdu):** حکمتِ عملی
* **Example:** We should create a strategy for scaling our system.

---

#### 🗣️ Idiom of the Day

**Under the weather**

* **Meaning (English):** Feeling unwell
* **Meaning (Urdu):** طبیعت ٹھیک نہ ہونا
* **Example:** I’m a bit under the weather today, so I’ll rest.

---

#### 🔁 Phrasal Verb of the Day

**Set up**

* **Meaning (English):** To arrange or establish something
* **Meaning (Urdu):** ترتیب دینا / قائم کرنا
* **Examples:**

  * I will set up a new server today.
  * The team set up the CI/CD pipeline.
